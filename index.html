What’s happening?

1 · Reflect

Light from Cody bounces off the mirror into Tommy’s eyes.

2 · Extend

The dashed extension behind the mirror locates Cody’s virtual image.

3 · Check

The incident and reflected angles are equal, measured from the normal.

Try this investigation

Drag Cody sideways until Tommy just loses sight of her. Notice where the ray would meet the mirror’s edge. Then drag her back until the reflection returns.

Drag Tommy and Cody around the larger grid, or move the mirror horizontally and vertically. Only rays that strike the mirror can reflect.
